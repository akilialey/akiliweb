Questions about the services

Do you write proposals?

The Bid Desk leads proposals for bids that have passed a Bid Economics Review. Standalone proposal writing isn’t offered, but we’re happy to point you to someone who does it.

How are fees set?

Every engagement is a fixed fee agreed before work starts. No hourly billing.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. Canadian clients are billed in CAD, US clients in USD.

Is my information safe?

Yes. No subcontractors, and never two clients on the same opportunity.
