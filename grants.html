What we do
The Bid Desk helps Canadian businesses and nonprofits find grant programs worth applying for — and write applications that actually get funded. Whether it's your first grant or your fifth, the right program and the right application make all the difference.
Track record

The same evaluation instincts that win government contracts — applied to grant funding.

Grant applications are judged the same way RFPs are: against defined criteria, by reviewers working through a stack of submissions looking for reasons to say yes — or no. The skills that win contracts — matching your capability to what's being asked, writing clearly, meeting every requirement — are the same skills that win grants.

I bring that same rigour to grant research and writing: finding programs you actually qualify for, and building applications reviewers can say yes to.

Why funding gets missed

Why good businesses miss out on funding they qualify for

01
The needle in the haystack

Too many programs, no map

There are hundreds of federal, provincial, municipal, and private grant programs in Canada. Finding the ones you actually qualify for shouldn't be a part-time job — but for most business owners, it is.

02
The blank page problem

You don't have time to write a strong application

Grant applications ask the same kinds of questions RFPs do — and take just as long to answer well. Between running the business and everything else, the application gets rushed, or it doesn't get written at all.

03
The compliance trap

Missed criteria kill applications before they're read

Grant reviewers work from a checklist just like procurement evaluators do. Miss one eligibility requirement or supporting document, and the strongest idea in Canada doesn't get funded.

04
The after-the-money problem

Winning the grant is only half the job

Most grants come with reporting obligations — and missing them can put future funding at risk. Post-award reporting is where a lot of grantees quietly fall behind.
