About The Bid Desk

One desk.
Every funding path.

The Bid Desk is a Canadian proposal management and procurement consultancy built for small and mid-sized businesses that want to compete for government contracts and grant funding — and keep winning them.

Some clients come with a single bid or application and need an expert review before it goes in. Others have been pursuing funding for years and can't figure out why their win rate isn't climbing. Both problems are solvable — the approach is the same: understand the full picture, build a real plan, and execute without shortcuts.

There are no junior consultants here. No rotating teams. When you work with The Bid Desk, you get a single experienced practitioner who is accountable from start to finish.
