Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Email akili@thebiddesk.ca. Every message gets a personal reply.

What does an RFP consultant do?

We help you decide which RFPs and tenders to bid on, what to charge, and how to position your proposal before any writing starts. When a bid passes that test, we can write and manage the full response.

Do you write proposals?

Yes. We write and manage RFP responses, after a Bid/No-Bid & Pricing Review or on their own when capacity allows. Starting with a review usually gets you more, because it tells you whether the proposal is worth writing, and the review fee is credited if you go ahead.

We already have a proposal team. Do we still need you?

Yes, if they spend time on bids that don’t pay. The work happens before they start: which RFPs to pursue and what to charge. They get fewer, better proposals to write.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is a fixed fee agreed before any work starts. Send the RFP and you get a quote within one business day. The 20-minute Bid Health Check is free.

Will it pay for itself?

No one honest can promise a win rate. Here’s how it usually pays off:

  • Skip one RFP you shouldn’t chase and keep weeks of senior time.
  • Price one big tender properly and protect your margin for years.
  • Fix the proposal section that keeps losing points and every bid gets stronger.
Can you guarantee we’ll win the contract?

No. Nobody honest can. We make sure you bid on the right RFPs, at the right price, with a proposal built to score.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. We work with Canadian firms bidding on US RFPs and US firms bidding on Canadian tenders, as well as contractors bidding at home.

Is my bid information confidential?

Yes. Everything stays between us. No subcontractors, and never two clients on the same opportunity.
