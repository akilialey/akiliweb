Four bids I see all the time.

Each looks like a writing problem. None of them is.

The incumbent you can’t beat, yet

A $2M services contract that fits you perfectly. The same vendor has held it for eight years, and past performance carries much of the score.

My callSkip this round. Get on the buyer’s radar before re-tender and be the obvious alternative.

The pursuit that costs more than the prize

A $300,000 RFP needing a live demo, two site visits, a teaming partner and legal review. The pursuit could cost more than it earns.

My callBid only if the demo and partner help you win other work. Otherwise, pass.

The win that costs money

Fixed price, 60-day payment terms, a holdback, and staff hired before day one. You fund payroll for months and the 12% margin shrinks fast.

My callBid, but price in the cash cost and ask for better payment terms in the question period.

The losses that look like price

Four losses in two years, each by a few points, each on the same rated section. The team kept cutting price.

My callStop cutting price and fix the one section that keeps losing points.

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Email akili@thebiddesk.ca. I answer every one myself.

What does an RFP or bid consultant do?

I help you decide which RFPs and tenders to bid on, what to charge, and how to position your bid before any writing starts. When a bid passes that test, I can lead the full response.

We already have a proposal team. Do we still need you?

Yes, if they spend time on bids that don’t pay. I work before they start: which opportunities to pursue and what to charge. They get better bids to write.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is a fixed fee agreed before any work starts. Send the opportunity and you get a quote within one business day. The 20-minute Bid Health Check is free.

Will it pay for itself?

I won’t promise a win rate. Here’s how it usually pays off:

  • Skip one bid you shouldn’t chase and keep weeks of senior time.
  • Price one big tender properly and protect your margin for years.
  • Fix the section that keeps losing points and every bid gets stronger.
Can you guarantee we’ll win the contract?

No. Nobody honest can. I make sure you chase the right bids, at the right price, as prepared as possible.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. Most clients are Canadian. I also help Canadian firms bidding on US opportunities and US firms bidding on Canadian tenders.

Is my bid information confidential?

Yes. Everything stays between us. No subcontractors, and never two clients on the same opportunity.
