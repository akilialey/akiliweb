Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Email akili@thebiddesk.ca. Every message gets a personal reply.

What does an RFP or bid consultant do?

The Bid Desk helps you decide which RFPs and tenders to bid on, what to charge, and how to position your bid before any writing starts. When a bid passes that test, we can lead the full response.

We already have a proposal team. Do we still need you?

Yes, if they spend time on bids that don’t pay. The work happens before they start: which opportunities to pursue and what to charge. They get better bids to write.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is a fixed fee agreed before any work starts. Send the opportunity and you get a quote within one business day. The 20-minute Bid Health Check is free.

Will it pay for itself?

No one honest can promise a win rate. Here’s how it usually pays off:

  • Skip one bid you shouldn’t chase and keep weeks of senior time.
  • Price one big tender properly and protect your margin for years.
  • Fix the section that keeps losing points and every bid gets stronger.
Can you guarantee we’ll win the contract?

No. Nobody honest can. The Bid Desk makes sure you chase the right bids, at the right price, as prepared as possible.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. Most clients are Canadian, and The Bid Desk also helps Canadian firms bidding on US opportunities and US firms bidding on Canadian tenders.

Is my bid information confidential?

Yes. Everything stays between us. No subcontractors, and never two clients on the same opportunity.
